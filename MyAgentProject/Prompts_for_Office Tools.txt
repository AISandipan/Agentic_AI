

### 1. Business Report

Create a new blank document.

Look for **Copilot** in the Word interface.

Try:

> Create a professional three-page business report about improving customer satisfaction. Include an executive summary, current challenges, proposed solutions, implementation plan, KPIs and conclusion.

You can then ask Copilot to:

* Make it more professional
* Add a table
* Summarize the report
* Create an executive summary
* Make the recommendations more detailed

---

### ⭐ Bonus: One powerful general Copilot prompt

You can use this with almost any existing Word document:

> Review this document and improve its structure, clarity and professional tone. Preserve the original meaning and important information. Remove unnecessary repetition, improve headings and formatting, and make the document easier for a business audience to read.
